# HomeloanCalc
A smart, mobile-friendly Home Loan Calculator that compares higher EMI, target payoff, and extra-EMI strategies. See how each option can change your loan maturity date, shorten repayment time, and reduce estimated interest.

🏠 Home Loan Calculator

A mobile-friendly Home Loan Repayment Calculator that helps you compare different strategies for closing your loan earlier.
Calculate the impact of higher monthly EMIs, extra annual EMI payments, or a target payoff year, and instantly estimate your revised maturity date, repayment time saved, remaining interest, and potential interest savings.
